# Systems Engineering course project

This repo houses work on a university course in Systems Engineering.
It is a 6-person project, with backgrounds in computer science, embedded systems, computer engineering, mechanical engineering, and electrical engineering.

We will not be actually implementing, but we will be making a detailed specification of the project.
Each week we will go further down the systems engineering steps and specify in more detail. We present our progress at the end of the week.
There is also a mid-term report. The final report is as if we were pitching to investors.

# Topic: Home Care For the Elderly

Out topic is "Home care for the elderly". The question we are trying to answer is "How can we allow elderly people to live at home longer before moving to a nursing home?".
The budget is 5000 EUR per person.
