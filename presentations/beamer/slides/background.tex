\begin{frame}{Data Background: Cognitive Decline in Older Adults}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Cognitive decline is a growing concern as populations age.

		\item LASA has followed $\sim$5,000 adults aged 55+ since 1992: cognition declines with age, though the rate varies between individuals\cite{lasa2025}.

		\item Older adults (75+) visit GPs most for memory/concentration issues; NL visits for this rose 24\% (2023 vs.\ 2020)\cite{rivm2023}.

		\item Across Europe, cognitive decline increases dependency on caregivers, straining independent living and healthcare systems\cite{formanek2019}.
	\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
